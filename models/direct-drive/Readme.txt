52 mm direct drive lens cap | CAD layout v3

Open Direct_Drive.scad in OpenSCAD with the supplied original STL beside it.
Use part = "sweep" to compare the 0, 45, 90, and 135 degree positions.
Use part = "assembly" and display_angle to inspect one pose.
Once fit dimensions are finalized, choose "base" or "cap" to render and
export either of the two solids. This package is for design review.

The original screw-in interface is imported at full size. The old hinge
and latch are trimmed outside the ring through the full imported depth.
A tapered, rounded web spreads the load around an arc of the base ring.
The cradle footing is now 6.5 mm wide. Both screw bosses taper into
a raised rail on the shaft side, with a clearance cut for the output wheel.
The wider portion lies below the nominal servo case.
The shaft position, smooth hub and 135 degree target are unchanged.
The cap has a continuous arm and a screw-clamped hub with a circular recess.
The servo is shown in the CAD as an approximate envelope.

Before ordering the prints
* Replace the four hub placeholders with the actual seat and screw dimensions.
* Confirm servo tab spacing, the tab-to-shaft offsets, and body clearance.
* Check where the mount ends up when the threaded base is snug, and check
  full focus travel with that orientation and a slack loop in the wire.
* Check the controller endpoints so the motor does not press against the rim.
